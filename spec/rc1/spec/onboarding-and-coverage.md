# Onboarding, goals and the supported-environment contract

Onboarding establishes a tenant, its accountable owner, business priorities, data purposes, working calendar and allowed intervention classes without asking the customer to design anything. Discovery authority, implementation authority and training authority are separate grants, and coverage tier is decided before a build starts (PR-016).

## 1. Onboarding state machines

| Principal | States and transitions | Required inputs | May remain unknown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | INVITED → VERIFIED (identity) → PRIORITIES_SET (goals, calendar, allowed intervention classes, spend caps) → ENVELOPE_DRAFTED → ENVELOPE_ACTIVE (data_use approval) → ACTIVE | Legal entity, accountable owner, goals as plain priorities, period calendar, the processors the firm already uses | Which systems hold which records (inventory finds out), org chart (inferred from operational relationships), policy details (asked only when a plan depends on them) |
| Employee | NOTIFIED (written notice per jurisdiction template) → ACKNOWLEDGED → ENROLLED (optional capture scope chosen) → ACTIVE; PAUSED and WITHDRAWN at any time | Acknowledgement; capture scope if enrolled | Nothing else; enrolment is never a condition of work |
| Client grantor | REQUESTED (consent request generated by the build) → CONSENTED (Plaid Link or portal share) → ACTIVE; REVOKED | Consent through the provider's own flow | Unsupported banks fall back to document requests |
| Plumb operator | ASSIGNED → ONBOARDING_REVIEWED (inventory and coverage decision countersigned) → MONITORING | None from the customer | None |

Every transition writes an audit row with actor and reason; notices and acknowledgements are stored as EvidenceEvents of modality interview with protection class CLEAR for their text.

## 2. Three authorities

| Authority | Object | Grants | Does not grant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | SourceGrant with purpose discovery, retention ≤ 90 days by default, processors limited to Plumb's own labeling path | Profiling, sampling, inventory probes, hypothesis evidence | Long retention, training, operation, export |
| Implementation and operation | AutonomyEnvelope plus SourceGrants with purposes implementation and operation; implement approval when impact exceeds the ceiling | Collectors, adapters, workflows, dispatch within scope | Training |
| Training | SourceGrant purpose training (and evaluation) per source, with the provider's terms version recorded | Dataset construction, fine-tuning, pooled evaluation | Cross-customer reuse (needs its own basis) |

Exploratory profiling runs under discovery authority before any opportunity is selected; its retention is bounded and nothing it collects enters a training set.

Eligibility rule: the coverage tier is assigned from the inventory and the SolutionSpec before execution (CoverageDecision with criteria_version). Coverage expansion (a new certified operation) is reported separately from improvements within an already certified environment.

## 4. Outcomes

Operating intervention, waiting for external dependency, terminal implementation failure, no beneficial intervention found, infeasible intervention, retired or superseded proposal (PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md §1). Non-delivery states remain in denominators.

## 5. Support boundary

Release 1 certifies the operations above. For anything else the architecture still runs: the inventory records the gap, the Sourcer searches compositions and adaptations, the Builder climbs the connector ladder and files a dependency when authority or a provider interface is missing. The customer is never asked to build the missing part; the platform team adds primitives and tests when existing interfaces are insufficient, and that labour is recorded as platform_engineering.

## 6. Omissions

Multi-entity tenants (a holding company with several legal entities) are represented as business entities within one tenant; billing between entities is not specified.
